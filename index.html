Understanding NDIS

Three Ways NDIS Funds Your Life

The NDIS provides flexible funding across three categories — designed to support your independence, equipment needs, and skill development.

Core Supports
Everyday Support
The most flexible category — covering personal care, household tasks, community access, and transport. Often used across categories to suit your needs.
  • Personal care & daily activities
  • Community access & transport
  • Domestic assistance
  • Respite & short-term accommodation
Capital Supports
Equipment & Home Modifications
One-off investments creating lasting independence — mobility aids, assistive technology, home modifications, and vehicle adaptations.
  • Mobility aids & wheelchairs
  • Assistive technology devices
  • Home & vehicle modifications
  • Supported Independent Living (SIL)
Capacity Building
Skills & Independence
Funding for therapy, training, and support coordination — helping you develop skills, find employment, and participate more fully in community life.
  • Support coordination
  • Therapy & allied health
  • Employment & life skills support
  • Social & community programs

Do I Qualify for the NDIS?

You need to meet the criteria below. Not sure if you're eligible? Contact us — we'll guide you through every step and explore all your options.

🇦🇺
Residency

Australian citizen, permanent resident, or Protected Special Category Visa holder

🎂
Age

Under 65 years old at the time of first applying for NDIS funding

♿
Disability

Permanent disability that significantly affects everyday activities and functioning

👶
Early Intervention

Under 9 with developmental delay or a condition likely to cause disability

📍
Location

Live in an NDIS area — we serve Darwin, Palmerston & surrounds
