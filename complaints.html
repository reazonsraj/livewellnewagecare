Complaints &
Feedback

Your feedback helps us improve. Everyone has the right to raise a concern or make a complaint — and to be heard with respect and without fear.

Our Commitment

Live Well Care And Support welcomes feedback and complaints as an opportunity to improve. We are committed to a complaints process that is fair, accessible, confidential, and free from retribution — consistent with the NDIS Practice Standards and the NDIS Code of Conduct.

You will never be disadvantaged for making a complaint, and we will support you to do so in whatever way works best for you.

How to Make a Complaint

You can raise a concern or complaint in any of the following ways:

  • By phone: 1300 934 541
  • By email: admin@livewellsupport.com.au
  • In writing: 36 Deane Crescent, Rosebery NT 0832
  • In person, by speaking with any member of our team
  • Through our contact form

You may ask a family member, friend, advocate, or interpreter to support you at any stage. If you need help to make a complaint, just let us know.

What Happens Next

We take every complaint seriously and handle it promptly and fairly:

  • We acknowledge your complaint within 2 business days
  • We work with you to understand the issue and what outcome you are seeking
  • We investigate fairly and keep you informed of progress
  • We aim to resolve complaints within 21 days, and explain any delay
  • We use what we learn to improve our services

If You're Not Satisfied

If we cannot resolve your complaint, or you would prefer to raise it externally, you can contact the relevant body directly at any time:

  • NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commission — 1800 035 544 or ndiscommission.gov.au
  • Aged Care Quality and Safety Commission — 1800 951 822 or agedcarequality.gov.au
  • Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (privacy matters) — 1300 363 992 or oaic.gov.au

Your Rights

You have the right to be treated with dignity and respect, to have your complaint handled confidentially, to involve a support person or advocate, and to receive a clear response. Making a complaint will never affect the quality of support you receive from us.
