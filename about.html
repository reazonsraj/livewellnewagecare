Who We Are

People-First Care
From Darwin

Live Well Care And Support is a nationally registered NDIS provider based in Darwin, Northern Territory. We also provide aged care services under the Australian Government's new Support at Home program — delivering high-quality, person-centred support to people with disability, older Australians, and their families.

We were built on a simple idea: that every person — regardless of age, ability, or background — deserves support that respects their dignity, upholds their choices, and helps them live the life they want. That idea still drives everything we do.

We serve participants and clients across Darwin, Palmerston, Tiwi, and Casuarina, delivering everything from daily personal care to support coordination, community participation, and home maintenance.
